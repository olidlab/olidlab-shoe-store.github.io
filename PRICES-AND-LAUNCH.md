# Before Launching OLIDLAB

**Storefront status: DEMO ONLY.** No live checkout, inventory, shipping, customer-service staffing, or verified OLIDLAB selling prices.

**Phone:** +1 (202) 555-0147 is a reserved **fictional** sample telephone number; it is not a functional support line. Replace it in `store-config.js`.

**Catalog prices:** The $ amounts are observed third-party retail price references where `verified=true`, or estimates where `verified=false`. They can change. The model, selected color and current sale price may differ from the retailer's general model price. Use each item's source link to verify before publishing retail prices.

**Images:** Product photos are direct URLs from external third-party publishers. Internet required; if an external image is blocked, a local graphic fallback is displayed. Do not represent photos as licensed or locally owned; obtain permission or replace with licensed assets before commercial deployment.

**For production:** supply a real number, confirmed inventory, licenses/image rights, checkout processor, legal business contact and genuine shipping/return/privacy policies.
